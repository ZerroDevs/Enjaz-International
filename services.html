Complete Objectives and Services

At ENJAZ, our primary objectives and goals cover all 15 core operational activities:

  • 1. Manufacturing and Production

    We manufacture and produce equipment for the oil and gas industry, along with their accessories, ensuring top-notch quality and reliability. Our products are distributed both locally and internationally.

  • 2. Medical Supplies

    We import a wide range of medical supplies, catering to the needs of healthcare providers and institutions.

  • 3. Oilfield Chemicals

    We import and supply essential chemicals required for oilfield operations, supporting the efficient functioning of the industry.

  • 4. Laboratory Equipment

    Our company imports and supplies specialized equipment for scientific laboratories, including medical, chemical, and environmental labs.

  • 5. Scientific Literature

    We provide all necessary scientific books related to our specialties, ensuring our clients have access to the latest knowledge and research.

  • 6. HVAC and Electronics

    We import, supply, install, and maintain air conditioning, refrigeration, and electronic equipment, offering comprehensive solutions for diverse needs.

  • 7. Firefighting Equipment

    We import, supply, and install top-tier firefighting equipment to ensure safety and compliance with regulations.

  • 8. Transportation of Oil and Gas

    We import and supply the necessary services for the transportation and movement of oil and gas, ensuring efficiency and safety.

  • 9. Freight Forwarding and Logistics

    We offer services for the transportation, loading, and unloading of oil and gas products, streamlining logistics and operations.

  • 10. Refining Equipment

    Our company imports oil and gas refining equipment and its accessories, carrying out all necessary work for its operation and maintenance.

  • 11. Freight Services

    We provide comprehensive freight forwarding, loading, and unloading services to meet the needs of our clients.

  • 12. Petroleum Stations

    We open and manage petroleum stations and their accessories, offering complete solutions for fuel distribution and related services.

  • 13. Specialized Transportation

    We supply specialized oil and gas transportation services, ensuring safe and efficient delivery.

  • 14. Oil and Gas Distribution

    We are involved in the supply and distribution of oil and gas, meeting the energy needs of various sectors.

  • 15. Partnerships and Collaborations

    Our company engages in all necessary and related activities to achieve our objectives. We are open to partnerships and collaborations with other entities, both locally and internationally, to enhance our operations and expand our reach.
